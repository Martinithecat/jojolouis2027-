Getting There

on our way!

By train

The nearest station is Headcorn, a 15 minute drive from the venue. Trains run from London Charing Cross and London Bridge a few times each hour.

There is no taxi rank at Headcorn station, so please book a taxi ahead if you're coming by train. Some local taxi companies:

  • Mazi Taxis: 07494 930977
  • MTC: 01622 890003
  • St Michaels & Tenterden Taxis: 01580 762277

By car

There is no parking at the venue, so please leave your car at your accommodation. We can give you directions to car parks in town, just ask!
